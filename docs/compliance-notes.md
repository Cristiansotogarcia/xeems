# Compliance Notes

## Deployment Posture

XEEMS is configured here for company-owned devices with written employee notification. The product is not operating on an in-app consent gate in this deployment.

## Minimum Operating Guardrails

- Employees receive written XEEMS notice before deployment
- Desktop monitoring remains visible and attributable, not covert
- Desktop tracking starts from admin-managed enrollment and persists until admin disablement
- Mobile tracking stays limited to active shifts
- No microphone or camera capture is part of the tracked scope
- Admin access remains separate from employee-facing apps

## Review Topics

- Written notice content and acknowledgment process
- Jurisdiction-specific labor and workplace monitoring rules
- Data retention windows and deletion policy
- Cross-border transfer and subprocessors
- Company-device policy wording for laptop and phone enrollment

# Compliance + Consent Notes

## Positioning
This product must be sold and operated as a transparent field-operations tool, not a covert employee surveillance product.

## Minimum compliance posture for MVP
- Clear pre-consent disclosure before enabling tracking
- Tracking active only during explicit work contexts (shift start to shift end)
- Worker-visible status showing whether tracking is active
- Written policy covering purpose, recipients, retention, and worker rights
- Ability to end tracking by ending shift
- Short data retention by default, reviewed with counsel per region

## Implementation guardrails
- No hidden background startup on app launch
- No off-shift ping collection
- No mic/camera access for tracking feature
- No silent escalation of permissions without explanation
- Separate admin access from worker self-view

## Recommended legal review topics
- Employee consent validity in relevant jurisdictions
- Legitimate-interest vs consent basis by country
- Retention limits and deletion requests
- Works council / labor law implications where relevant
- Location data transfer and subprocessors
